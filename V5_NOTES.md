Goal: validate the emotional core before expanding the full piece.

- ~20 bars at 84 BPM, roughly one minute.
- Main motif appears immediately.
- Build starts within the opening bars.
- Climax occupies most of the second half.
- Left hand grows from flowing arpeggios into octave-supported bass.
- Keeps the V3 sampled-grand engine unchanged.
- Short resolution only; no long intro or outro.
